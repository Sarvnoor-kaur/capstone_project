# UI/UX Guidelines

## Goals
Calm, clear, accessible interface. Do not imitate a diagnostic portal or make exaggerated promises. Avoid manipulative engagement patterns, streak pressure or alarming design.

## Student areas
Dashboard, AI chat, screening, mood tracking, approved resources, referral request/status.

## Counsellor areas
Assigned referrals, referral details limited to authorized information, status updates and permitted follow-up notes.

## Admin areas
Account/counsellor management, resource approval/review, operational metrics without unnecessary private-content exposure.

## Interaction rules
Explain form purpose; show inline validation; confirm destructive actions; provide loading/empty/success/failure states; never show success when AI failed; explain consent/sharing scope; label AI-generated support; do not call screening results diagnoses.

## Accessibility
Keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, semantic HTML, labels, adequate contrast, responsive layout, and no reliance on color alone. Target WCAG 2.2 AA where practical.

## Privacy
Avoid private chat text in shared dashboards, notification previews, URLs, analytics events or client logs.
