## 📊 10 Different Chart Types

### **Layout Structure:**

#### **1. Hero Stats Cards** (Top)
- 4 stat cards with icons
- Total Entries, Average Mood, Streak, Trend
- Already existed, now styled better

#### **2. Two-Column Section**
**Left: Donut Chart** - Mood Distribution
- Beautiful circular chart with percentages
- Color-coded segments with emojis
- Interactive hover effects

**Right: Radar Chart** - 360° Mood Balance
- Pentagon radar visualization
- Shows frequency of each mood type
- Perfect for pattern recognition

#### **3. Full-Width Area Chart**
- 30-Day Mood Journey
- Smooth gradient area fill
- Purple to blue gradient
- Dots on data points

#### **4. Three-Column Cards**
**Col 1: Frequency Chart** - Most Common Moods
- Vertical bars
- Top 3 moods highlighted

**Col 2: Time Patterns** - Daily Analysis
- Colorful bars for different times
- Morning/Afternoon/Evening/Night

**Col 3: Gauge Chart** - Wellness Score
- Semicircular gauge (0-100%)
- Color changes based on score
- Visual health indicator

#### **5. Two-Column Comparison**
**Left: Scatter Plot** - Entry Distribution
- Colored dots over time
- Each dot is a mood entry
- Shows patterns and outliers

**Right: Weekly Comparison** - Bar + Line Combo
- Last 4 weeks comparison
- Gradient green bars
- Entry count overlay

#### **6. Full-Width Heatmap**
- 12-week calendar view
- Color-coded days
- Hover tooltips
- Pattern recognition

#### **7. Monthly Summary Line Chart**
- 6 months of data
- 3 lines: Average, Highest, Lowest
- Gradient and dashed lines
- Long-term trends
